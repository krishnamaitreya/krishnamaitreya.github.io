Hi, I’m Krishna.

I am currently pursuing my Ph.D. in the Department of Computer Science and Information Systems at BITS Pilani, Hyderabad Campus, under the supervision of Prof. Paresh Saxena. My research centers on developing edge-efficient computer vision models for real-time surveillance, with a particular focus on multi-task learning architectures that allow a single model to perform multiple vision tasks efficiently. As part of a DRDO-funded project titled Computer Vision-Based Search and Surveillance System for Detecting Human Activity, I am building vision systems that detect and analyze human activity in surveillance footage while remaining lightweight enough to run on edge hardware. My broader interest lies at the intersection of computer vision, edge AI, and resource-constrained intelligent systems.

Before beginning my Ph.D., I worked as a Graduate Engineer Trainee at Eazythings Technology Pvt. Ltd., where I developed IoT-enabled smart energy monitoring and metering products. This involved designing energy measurement systems around Analog Devices metering ICs, including the ADE7753, ADE7758, and ADE9000, to accurately measure electrical parameters such as energy, power, voltage, current, and power quality. The role gave me hands-on experience across circuit design, PCB development, embedded systems, and IoT product development, including work with the Raspberry Pi.

The smart energy metering technology I helped build has since been patented and deployed in industrial settings, including at Ashok Leyland.
